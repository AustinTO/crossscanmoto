# Official compatibility references

Retrieved 2026-10-30.

- https://www.motoscan.de/docs/allgemein/erforderliches-zubehoer/ recommends UniCarScan UCSI-2000 and OBDLink LX/MX+/MX Bluetooth for unrestricted functions. Generic ELM327 compatible interfaces may be restricted; Wi-Fi interfaces are not recommended by the vendor. Older non-Euro4 motorcycles may require a 16-to-10 pin cable.
- https://www.motoscan.de/docs/allgemein/unterstuetzte-motorraeder/ documents model coverage and special ABS adapter requirements.
- https://www.motoscan.de/docs/allgemein/inbetriebnahme/ documents Android pairing and distinguishes UniCarScan from the separately advertised UniCarScan iOS device.
- https://www.motoscan.de/faq/ states MotoScan itself is Android only.

These sources describe MotoScan compatibility. They do not validate this Flutter reconstruction. Flutter platform support does not imply the same adapter can communicate on every operating system.
